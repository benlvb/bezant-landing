# What is Bezant?

Bezant is a private, on-device personal finance app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It helps you plan where money should go and log where it went — in one tap.

**No account required. No bank linking. No ads.** Your numbers stay on your device. Optional iCloud sync uses *your* CloudKit private database; Bezant does not run a server that stores your finances.

## What you can do
- **Log spending fast** — in-app Quick Log, Home Screen widgets, Siri, or Apple Watch. Keep a daily streak.
- **Plan goals** — savings goals with Top / Mid / Low priority and funded-by dates. Use **What-if** on Home to preview extra contributions or cuts without committing them.
- **Track trips and time-bounded spend** — Events for day-bounded trips and similar budgets (manual entry, not bank import).
- **Stay private** — hide amounts, Face Unlock, app-switcher blur.

## What Bezant is not
- Not a bank-linked aggregator (no Plaid / Open Banking).
- Not an AI budget coach or credit-score product.
- Not a crypto wallet.
